Nate Treat is a forward thinking and art-inspired 20 year-old student, raised in Oregon and currently based in Tokyo as an exchange student. Growing up, Nate discovered himself through his love of stories—whether it was video games, movies or music. And although it isn’t his main focus in school, Nate is constantly sharing stories through creating videos, writing music and even coding this website. It’s this desire to understand the bigger picture that led him to study business, and specifically accounting.

Nate plans to start his career with an internship at PwC Audit during the summer of 2026 in Los Angeles. As someone who’s always looking to globalize his network, he knows LA is the best place to start. He understands that change doesn’t come from staticity, and there’s always something to learn from where we are.
